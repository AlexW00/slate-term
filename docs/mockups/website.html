05 / Mechanism + iteration

Use it. Change it. Keep going.

Slate’s powerful plugin system and CLI give you and your AI agents the tools to create custom panels, commands, and interfaces—right where you work.

  1. Build

    Work with your agent to turn an idea into a working tool.

  2. Use

    Bring it into your workspace and try it on the real task.

  3. Refine

    Adjust what’s missing. Keep what works. Repeat as your needs change.

Your agent builds. Slate connects the pieces.
Plugins provide the interfaces and commands. The CLI lets your agent register tools, arrange panes, and interact with the workspace.
